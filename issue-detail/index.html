AI Summary

The Election Commission has announced a Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of voter rolls in Bihar. The exercise aims to identify and remove duplicate, ineligible, and fake entries, and to ensure that only eligible citizens are included in the voter list. The process will be conducted across all districts of Bihar and has sparked political debate, with opposition parties raising concerns about possible exclusion of genuine voters.

Why Does It Matter?

A clean and accurate voter list is essential for free and fair elections. The revision can impact voter turnout, political representation, and the credibility of the electoral process in Bihar.
